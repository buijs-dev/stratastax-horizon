The meeting point of Earth and Sky. 
> Where the architecture of Stratastax meets the world—the horizon where our technology and its users first connect.

## Overview
Stratastax Horizon is the public face of the Stratastax ecosystem. 
Built on the edge with plain HTML and CSS, 
hosted on Cloudflare Pages, 
it serves as the atmospheric entry point for our users, 
offering a high-performance, content-rich experience.

While the engine powers the logic and the portal facilitates the work, Horizon provides the vision.

## Relationship to Ecosystem
- stratastax-engine: The core service providing the data. 
- stratastax-portal: The Flutter-based application for authenticated users. 
- stratastax-infrastructure: The Nomad + Terraform blueprints that keep the lights on. 
- stratastax-horizon: This repository. The public interface.
